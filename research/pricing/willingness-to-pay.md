# Willingness To Pay — Analysis

Date: 2026-08-24. Based on competitor pricing (research/competitors/landscape.md) and
customer pain (research/customer-pain/evidence.md).

## What customers already pay for pieces of this problem
| Need | Current solution | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Attribution / blended ROAS | Triple Whale | $129–$749/mo |
| Rigorous MTA + MMM | Northbeam | $1,500–$5,000+/mo |
| Agency management | DTC/performance agency | $2K–$15K/mo retainer |
| Incrementality test (one-off) | Recast/Mesa/etc | $8K–$25K |
| MMM platform | custom | $3.5K–$15K/mo |
| AI ad MCP connector | Adspirer/PaidSync/NotFair | $0–$249/mo |

## Customer's total cost of the problem (annual)
- Ad waste: 15–50% of platform-claimed conversions are double-counted; at $50K/mo spend that
  is $7.5K–$25K/mo of misallocated or over-credited spend.
- Labor: 16–30 hrs/mo reconciliation (≈ $1K–$3K/mo of analyst time).
- Agency: $24K–$180K/yr.

## Our value levers (from brief Phase 10)
money saved (stop waste) + revenue generated (reallocate to winners) + labor reduced
(stop manual reconciliation).

## Pricing headroom
A product delivering:
  (a) honest cross-channel reconciliation (Northbeam-grade truth),
  (b) daily AI diagnosis + recommendation (Triple Whale Moby-grade),
  (c) safe approval-gated execution (Adspirer/PaidSync-grade),
at $99–$499/mo, sits BELOW every honest incumbent and ABOVE the thin MCP connectors. Clear
willingness to pay exists: the pain is documented, the alternatives are expensive or weak,
and the ROI (recovered wasted spend alone) dwarfs the price at any realistic ad spend.

## Suggested pricing dimensions (validate later, Phase 30)
- Primary: monthly ad spend tier (like Triple Whale/Northbeam) OR per-connected-account
  (like Pipeboard/NotFair). Spend-tier aligns price with value (more spend = more waste to
  recover = more willingness to pay).
- Tiers: Starter $99 (1 platform, 1 account, daily brief + recommendations, manual exec),
  Growth $249 (2 platforms, approval-gated exec, experiments), Agency $499+ (multi-account,
  multi-tenant, white-label). Per-task models (Adspirer) undervalue the outcome loop.
