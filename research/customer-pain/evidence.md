# Customer Pain — Evidence (not invented)

Captured 2026-08-24 via web_search across agency blogs, attribution vendors, and marketing
press. These are REAL, widely-documented pains. Use them to validate the wedge.

## 1. Attribution theatre / double-counting (strongest, most repeated)
- Platforms last-click-attribute by default. Meta + Google both claim the same purchase.
  Sum of platform-claimed conversions routinely EXCEEDS actual sales by 15–50%.
- Agencies sit behind the inflated combined ROAS number; founders see the gap only in P&L.
- Sources: btbaudits.com ("Attribution Theatre", $147M+ audited), slaymakermarketing.com
  (ROAS faking: branded search, long windows, view-through, platform-sum),
  engineersinmarketing.com (6 reporting tricks), softpath.co (CRM vs Meta 35% gap),
  databox.com (1 in 4 GTM leaders: ≥25% pipeline misattributed; 7% error >50%).

## 2. Platform-reported ROAS is 2.3x higher than ground truth
- 150-brand 2026 study (growwithba.com): Meta+Google+TikTok platform ROAS avg 2.3x higher
  than marginal ROAS from incrementality/holdout. Meta over-reported 2.1–3.5x, Google
  1.4–2.2x, PMax 2.8–4.1x (worst). 68% of those who ran incrementality found ≥1 channel
  with NEGATIVE incrementality.

## 3. Reconciliation is manual and slow
- Databox Time-to-Insight: 64% need 1–3 days to answer one business question; 32% spend
  16–30 hrs/mo just cleaning/reconciling attribution data before analysis.

## 4. Creative fatigue is invisible until ROAS drops
- CTR declining, frequency climbing, CPC rising = fatigue. Most operators catch it late.
  (GoMarble meta-creative-analysis, MattBerman meta-ads-kit both target this.)

## 5. Wasted spend is structural
- Branded search inflated into blended ROAS; non-branded prospecting hidden. View-through
  padding. Dead ad sets. Search-term waste. All well documented.

## 6. SMB/e-com cannot afford the honest tools
- Northbeam starts $1,500/mo; only justified at $250K+/mo media spend. Below that, the
  rigorous measurement is out of reach. Triple Whale free/cheap tier lacks MMM/incrementality.
  → Gap: honest attribution + AI action at SMB price.

## What customers SAY they want (derived)
- "Show me what actually happened to my money, not what Meta/Google claim."
- "Tell me what to change, and let me approve it safely."
- "Don't make me export CSVs and reconcile in a spreadsheet every week."
