# Landing Page Research (SaaS, 2026)

## Canonical 8-section structure
Hero -> social proof bar -> problem -> features (bento) -> how it works ->
testimonials -> pricing -> FAQ -> final CTA.

## Conversion benchmarks
- Bounce < 40%, time on page > 2 min, CTA click > 3%, signup 3-8% cold.
- Every 1s delay costs ~7% conversion. Mobile-first (60%+ traffic).
- Blur test: at 10-15px Gaussian blur, headline + CTA + product visual still show.
- 5-second test: a stranger can state what the product does in 5s.

## Kill-conversion anti-patterns
Vague hero, stock hero photo, CTA below fold, 3+ competing CTAs, feature lists
instead of problem/solution, hidden pricing, generic testimonials, no FAQ.

## PerfOS application
Landing page already built (apps/web/app/page.tsx) in a dark premium theme with
these exact sections. Keep the hero outcome-led: "Stop platforms inflating your
ROAS." CTA: "Start free trial". No em dashes in copy.
