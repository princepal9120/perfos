## Comparison to maturity
Design/video FOSS the user came from: OpenDesign ~90k, OpenMontage ~49k,
MoneyPrinterTurbo ~99k stars. The ads-MCP space has NOTHING close. Top repo 83.

## Fork in the road
- "Famous" ones (Adspirer 83, Synter 16) are **hosted/SaaS-backed** (API key).
  Opposite of the OpenDesign model (local, BYOK, agent-native, you own it).
- Genuinely local/BYOK ones (adport 4, mkt-cli 5) match the architecture but are
  essentially unknown. 4-5 stars = no community, likely rough edges.

## Conclusion
"Famous AND open/BYOK/local like OpenDesign" does not exist yet in ads. The
category has not produced its OpenDesign equivalent. Therefore PerfOS should BE
that thing: a local, BYOK, all-channels, policy-gated ads OS. Build it, don't
adopt a half-baked 4-star repo.
