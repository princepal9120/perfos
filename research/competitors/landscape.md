## Key observations
1. The category is CROWDED at the "MCP connector + AI agent" layer. Most competitors are
   hosted SaaS charging per-task or per-account. They are connectors + chat, NOT a product
   that owns the decision intelligence, attribution reconciliation, and outcome loop.
2. Incumbent attribution tools (Triple Whale / Northbeam) are EXPENSIVE ($150–$5,000/mo) and
   their own AI is either weak (Northbeam: "No AI") or dashboard-bound (Triple Whale: Moby
   answers questions but execution is limited).
3. Nobody in either group owns the "honest cross-channel truth vs platform-claimed numbers"
   wedge cleanly for the SMB/e-com operator who cannot afford Northbeam. This is the gap.

## Pricing evidence for our model
- Customers already pay: Triple Whale $129–$749/mo, Northbeam $1,500+/mo, agencies
  $2K–$15K/mo retainer, incrementality tests $8K–$25K, MMM platforms $3.5K–$15K/mo.
- Implication: a product that delivers "Northbeam-grade honesty at Triple Whale price, with
  AI actions" has clear willingness-to-pay headroom at $99–$499/mo.
